## 1. Project Background & Vision
Aurelis is an editorial frontend showcase website for a flagship acoustic headphone brand. Drawing direct inspiration from the Stitch visual reference (`/reference/design/img1.png` - `img5.png`), the project combines luxury industrial design presentation with dynamic scroll-driven choreography using React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and GSAP.

---

## 2. Narrative Arc & User Flow
1. **Prologue / Hero**: Moody, cinematic dark void, floating 3/4 perspective headphone, bold stacked typography, electric lime CTA pill, colorway finish toggles.
2. **Feature Architecture**: Split-screen technical specifications with interactive 5-item accordion and companion wireless charging case card.
3. **Ergonomic Anatomy**: Symmetrical frontal headphone elevation with 6 pulsing interactive hotspot markers and accessory switcher thumbnails.
4. **Product Suite**: Horizontal card deck with synchronized scroll track indicator and color swatch selectors.
5. **Editorial Endorsement**: Dual-tone quotation with carousel arrow navigation and 5-star reviewer card.
6. **Brand Mission & Trust**: Centered split-weight manifesto statement, lime CTA, and 6-item technology partner logo grid.
7. **Editorial Stories**: 3-column vertical article cards with photography, dates, and link interactions.
8. **Master Footer**: Newsletter subscription form, categorized navigation, social icons, and payment badges.
